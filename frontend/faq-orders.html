ORDER HELP

Information about placing, modifying, and cancelling your orders.

Can I modify my order after placing it?

We process orders quickly, but if you contact us within 30 minutes of placing your order, we may be able to make changes.

How do I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order from your Account dashboard if it hasn't been processed for shipping yet. Look for the "Cancel" button in your Order History.

What if I received a damaged item?

We're so sorry! Please take a photo of the damage and contact our Support team immediately. We'll arrange a replacement or refund right away.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes! You can add premium gift wrapping and a personalized message at the checkout stage.
