ACCOUNT HELP

Everything you need to know about managing your SHOP EASE profile.

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top banner or the profile icon in the navigation bar. Fill in your details, and you're ready to go!

How can I reset my password?

Go to the Login page and click "Forgot Password". We'll send you an email with instructions to securely reset your credentials.

Can I change my email address?

Yes, you can update your email address in the "Account Settings" section of your profile dashboard.

How do I delete my account?

We'd be sad to see you go! If you wish to delete your account, please contact our support team through the Support page.
