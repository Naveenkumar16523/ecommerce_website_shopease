DELIVERY HELP

Questions about shipping, tracking, and delivery timelines.

How can I track my delivery?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also see live updates in the "Orders" section of your account.

What happens if I'm not home?

Our couriers will usually leave a card or try to re-deliver the next day. You can often manage specific delivery instructions through the courier's website.

Can I change my delivery address?

If your order hasn't been dispatched yet, contact us immediately. Once it's with the courier, address changes may not be possible.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to over 150 countries. Delivery times and costs vary by location and are calculated at checkout.
